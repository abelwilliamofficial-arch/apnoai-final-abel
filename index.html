About us

A neighbourhood clinic that explains before it treats.

Smile Square was started by Dr. Meera Kulkarni with one promise: you will always know what is being done and why. We keep visits calm, use modern sterilised equipment, and never recommend a treatment you do not need. Over 5,000 patients, from toddlers to grandparents, now call us their family dentist.

Our services

Everything your family needs, under one roof.

Checkups and cleaning

A full exam, scaling and polish in 30 minutes. We recommend one visit every six months.

Braces and clear aligners

Metal braces for budgets, clear aligners for discretion. Free first consultation and a written plan.

Painless root canal

Single-sitting root canal with numbing gel and a quiet room, so you can leave without dread.

Teeth whitening

Safe in-clinic whitening that lifts tea, coffee and everyday stains in about an hour.

Dental implants

Permanent replacement for missing teeth, planned after a scan and a clear cost estimate.

Children's dentistry

Gentle first visits, fluoride care and fillings in a friendly setting for kids of all ages.
